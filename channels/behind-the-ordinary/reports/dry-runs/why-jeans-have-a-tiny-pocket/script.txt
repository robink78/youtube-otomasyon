That tiny jeans pocket was not designed for loose change. It is a surviving piece of nineteenth-century workwear. Levi Strauss and Company calls it the watch pocket. Pocket watches were common, so the small pouch carried and protected one. It appeared on the original four-pocket riveted waist overalls in 1873. The second back pocket arrived in 1901, making that the actual fifth pocket. So this tiny pocket is a working trace of the original jeans design.
