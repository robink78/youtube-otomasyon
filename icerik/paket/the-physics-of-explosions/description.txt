An explosion kills in a way most people misunderstand. It is not the flames - it is a wall of pressure moving faster than sound. The failure mechanism: blast overpressure.

Failure chain: Solid turns to gas → Shock front forms → Overpressure hits → Negative phase pulls back → Debris flies
