On January 28, 1986, Space Shuttle Challenger broke apart 73 seconds after launch. A cold-stiffened O-ring in a solid rocket booster joint failed to seal.

Failure chain: Cold night below freezing → O-rings lose resilience → Booster joint fails to seal → Flame burns into external tank → Vehicle breaks apart at 73 s
