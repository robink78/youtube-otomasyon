Every bridge is in a quiet war against physics - wind, resonance, and metal fatigue. Here is how bridges actually fail, and why the ones you cross survived.

Failure chain: Wind & traffic loads → Cyclic flexing → Fatigue cracks grow → Corrosion thins steel → Member fails
