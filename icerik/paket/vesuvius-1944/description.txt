In 1944, Mount Vesuvius erupted in the middle of World War II, burying villages and destroying dozens of Allied bombers. The failure mechanism: heavy ash fall and lava flows.

Failure chain: Magma recharges → Lava flows down slopes → Ash column rises → Hot ash falls on airfield → Dozens of B-25s wrecked
