In some earthquakes, solid ground turns to liquid and swallows buildings whole. Here is the eerie science. The failure mechanism: soil liquefaction.

Failure chain: Loose wet sand → Shaking pushes grains → Water pressure rises → Grains lose contact → Ground flows like liquid
