Everyone fears the elevator plunging - but the physics make it almost impossible. Here is why. The failure mechanism: redundant ropes and an overspeed safety brake.

Failure chain: Multiple steel ropes → Any one holds the car → Overspeed governor trips → Safety brakes grip rails → Buffers in the pit
