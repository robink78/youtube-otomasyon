Rust is a chemical fire in slow motion that has collapsed bridges and sunk ships. Here is how it wins. The failure mechanism: electrochemical corrosion.

Failure chain: Water + oxygen + salt → Iron oxidizes → Rust expands → Coating cracks → Section thins → failure
