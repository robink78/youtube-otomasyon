Rana Plaza collapsed with garment workers inside. The cause: overloaded structure with illegal floors, heavy machinery and generators on weak ground.

Failure chain: Built on a filled pond → Extra floors added illegally → Heavy generators on top floors → Cracks appear → Workers sent back; collapse
