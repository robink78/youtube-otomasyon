On June 15, 1904, the steamboat General Slocum burned in New York Harbor. Its life preservers were rotten and weighted with iron; its lifeboats were wired in place. The failure mechanism: safety equipment that passed inspection but did not work.

Failure chain: Fire starts in storage room → Rotten 13-year-old life preservers → Some weighted with iron to pass → Lifeboats wired to the deck → Inspection had passed it all
