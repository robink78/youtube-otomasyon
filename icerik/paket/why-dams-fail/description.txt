A dam holds back millions of tons of water - and it only takes one weak spot to unleash it all. Here is how dams fail. The failure mechanism: internal erosion (piping).

Failure chain: Seepage through embankment → Soil carried away → Hidden pipe forms → Breach opens → Flood wave downstream
