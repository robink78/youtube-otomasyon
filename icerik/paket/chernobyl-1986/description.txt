Chernobyl exploded during a safety test. The cause: positive void coefficient plus graphite-tipped control rods causing a power surge.

Failure chain: Safety test at low power → Unstable reactor state → Emergency shutdown pressed → Graphite tips spike power → Steam explosion destroys core
