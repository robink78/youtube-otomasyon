A rocket is a controlled explosion pointed at the sky - and the line between launch and disaster is razor thin. Here is why they blow up. The failure mechanism: uncontrolled propellant ignition and tank overpressure.

Failure chain: Tiny leak or stuck valve → Propellants mix wrong → Uncontrolled ignition → Tank overpressure → Vehicle breaks up
