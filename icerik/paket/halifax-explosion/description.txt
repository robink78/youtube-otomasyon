In 1917, the Halifax Explosion was the largest man-made blast before the atomic bomb. The failure mechanism: detonation of a burning munitions cargo.

Failure chain: Imo & mont-blanc collide → Benzol on deck catches fire → Crew abandons ship → 2.9-kiloton detonation → North end flattened
