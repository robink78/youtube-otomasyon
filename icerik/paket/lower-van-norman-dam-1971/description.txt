Van Norman Dam nearly failed in the San Fernando earthquake. The cause: liquefaction of hydraulic fill causing a landslide that took part of the crest.

Failure chain: Hydraulic fill Dam → Earthquake → Fill liquefies → Crest slides into reservoir → Emergency drawdown

Another reconstruction: The Disaster That Ended the Age of the Airship — https://youtu.be/PGOKZhPYo7c
