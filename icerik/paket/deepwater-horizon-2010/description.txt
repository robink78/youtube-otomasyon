On April 20, 2010, a blowout destroyed the Deepwater Horizon rig and killed 11 workers. A misread pressure test and a blowout preventer that could not seal the well turned it into the largest marine oil spill in history.

Failure chain: Cement barrier fails → Pressure test misread as pass → Gas rises to the rig → Gas ignites — rig explodes → Bop can't cut buckled pipe
