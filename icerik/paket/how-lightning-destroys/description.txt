Lightning is five times hotter than the sun and can blow concrete apart. Here is how it destroys. The failure mechanism: resistive heating and steam expansion.

Failure chain: Bolt strikes roof → Seeks fastest path down → Current flows through moisture → Water flashes to steam → Masonry bursts
